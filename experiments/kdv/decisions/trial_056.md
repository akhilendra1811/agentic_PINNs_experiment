# Trial 056 Decision Record: Targeted Tri-Harmonic Coordinate Projection ($k \in \{1, 2, 3\}$)

## Hypothesis
In the Korteweg-de Vries (KdV) equation:
$$u_t + u u_x + 0.022^2 u_{xxx} = 0$$
the convective term $u u_x$ drives continuous non-linear energy transfer from fundamental modes to higher-order spatial frequencies (harmonic generation).
When initial waves with frequencies $f_1 = 1$ and $f_2 = 2$ collide, the quadratic interaction yields:
$$u u_x \sim [a_1 \cos(\pi x) + a_2 \cos(2\pi x)] [-a_1 \pi \sin(\pi x) - 2 a_2 \pi \sin(2\pi x)]$$
The cross-term product $\cos(\pi x) \sin(2\pi x) = \frac{1}{2}(\sin(3\pi x) + \sin(\pi x))$ directly generates the third harmonic mode $k = 3$ ($\sin(3\pi x)$ and $\cos(3\pi x)$).
In incumbent Trial 033, the coordinate projection was restricted to dual harmonics $k \in \{1, 2\}$. Consequently, the network was forced to synthesize the non-linearly generated $k = 3$ frequency purely through deep non-linear layer compositions, which is susceptible to neural spectral bias.
By extending the coordinate mapping to include exact targeted tri-harmonic features ($k \in \{1, 2, 3\}$):
$$\Phi(x) = [\sin(\pi x), \cos(\pi x), \sin(2\pi x), \cos(2\pi x), \sin(3\pi x), \cos(3\pi x)]$$
we provide explicit linear access to the first generated collision harmonic.
This increases `in_dim` from 7 to 9, increasing parameters from 219,650 to 220,002 (well below the 250,000 parameter cap), with zero additional autograd complexity or throughput slowdown, testing whether direct 3rd-harmonic representation improves two-soliton collision resolution on `dev_02`.

## Training objective used
Augmented conservative physics-informed loss:
$$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PDE}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{IC}} \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{IC}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{inv}} \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{inv}}$$
with $\lambda_{\mathrm{IC}} = 1.0, \lambda_{\mathrm{inv}} = 0.10$, evaluated over $S=8$ stochastic time slices with $N_x=512$ spatial quadrature points per slice (4,096 invariant points per step), alongside 4,096 fresh uniform collocation points and 512 IC points per step.

## Expected effect
Direct linear availability of the $k = 3$ spatial harmonic enables the input projection layer to immediately transmit non-linear collision energy to hidden layers, mitigating spectral bias and sharpening soliton collision peaks on `dev_02`.
