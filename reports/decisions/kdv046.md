## Hypothesis
Fine-tuning the peak learning rate to $1.08 \times 10^{-3}$ (an 8% increase over baseline $1.0 \times 10^{-3}$, well below the $1.2 \times 10^{-3}$ threshold that overshot on dev_02 in Trial 041) will accelerate multiharmonic cross-coupling convergence on dev_03 and dev_01 without destabilizing dev_02's steep wave crest, pushing mean validation loss below Trial 045's 0.003210.

## Proposed method change
In candidate.py:
1. In `train_model`, set `LEARNING_RATE = 1.08e-3` (from 1.0e-3).
2. Retain all other winning baseline components from Trial 045:
   - ResNet with 8 hidden layers, width 160 (181,442 parameters)
   - Input representation: $[t, \sin(\pi x), \cos(\pi x), u_0(x), u_0'(x)/\pi]$
   - Learnable alpha timescale in $\tanh(\alpha t)$ exact IC envelope
   - 35% duration linear curriculum with 0.05 onset: $t_{scale} = 0.05 + 0.95 \cdot (fraction / 0.35)$
   - Curriculum-synchronized cosine schedule (lr=1.08e-3 during phase 1, decay to 1e-7 during phase 2)
   - AdamW with betas=(0.9, 0.99), weight_decay=5e-5.

## Training objective used
Total training objective is L_total = L_physics + L_ic (with L_ic evaluating to 0.0 identically) using LEARNING_RATE=1.08e-3, 35% curriculum horizon, synchronized 1e-7 floor cosine schedule, and 5e-5 weight decay.

## Expected effect
In Trial 041, a 20% learning rate increase (1.2e-3) yielded massive improvements on dev_01 (2.16e-6) and dev_03 (0.001634), but was slightly too aggressive for dev_02. A calibrated 8% increase (1.08e-3) will provide the extra gradient momentum needed to accelerate dev_03 without exceeding the stability limit on dev_02.

## Evidence from previous experiments
Trial 041 revealed that dev_03 error drops by 24% (from 0.00215 to 0.001634) with higher learning rate, while dev_01 drops to 2.16e-6. Moderating the increase from +20% to +8% targets the Pareto-optimal operating point for all three cases simultaneously.

## Agent decision
Adopt: Set peak LEARNING_RATE to 1.08e-3 as the new baseline default.

## Reason for decision
Calibrating the peak learning rate to 1.08e-3 yielded our greatest multi-case breakthrough, simultaneously breaking through the sub-0.007 barrier on dev_02 (0.006890), the sub-0.002 barrier on dev_03 (0.001957), and the global sub-0.003 threshold (mean loss 0.002950). An 8% increase over 1.0e-3 provided the exact optimizer authority needed to resolve nonlinear wave fission and multi-soliton collisions without triggering the step instability observed with 1.2e-3 in Trial 041. The setting LEARNING_RATE=1.08e-3 is adopted into the baseline.
